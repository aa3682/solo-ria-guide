# Tools

This section holds the pages you work with rather than read through: one table of every figure set by law, regulation, or an agency, and three worksheets tied to particular steps in [The Independent Path](/process). Each one is built to be opened at the point its step comes up, and each says plainly what its result does and does not settle.

## Worksheets, not explainers

A worksheet here is a working sheet you fill in with your own figures, not an explanation of a subject. [The Independent Path](/process) and [Practice Areas](/domains) do the explaining; a worksheet names what to gather, walks the arithmetic, and tells you where the answer stops being reliable.

## What's here

The figures table first, then the three worksheets in the order the steps reach them.

- **[This year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures)** holds every limit, rate, threshold, and deadline set by law, regulation, or an agency, each with its year and its source. It is updated once a year, and the rest of the site links here rather than printing a number of its own.
- **[Reaching Your Written Decision](/tools/reaching-your-written-decision)** works the runway behind a go or no-go as a range rather than a single figure, and is explicit that it does only the numbers half of the decision. It supports [Decide If Independence Fits](/process/decide-if-independence-fits).
- **[Calculating Your Regulatory Assets Under Management](/tools/calculating-your-regulatory-assets-under-management)** works out the assets figure that decides which regulator registers your firm, including what does not count toward it. It supports [Choose Your Regulator](/process/choose-your-regulator).
- **[Building Your Running Calendar](/tools/building-your-running-calendar)** assembles the filings and reviews a registered firm repeats — what recurs on the yearly cycle, and what fires on a trigger instead. It supports [Stay Registered and Compliant](/process/stay-registered-and-compliant).
