# This year's figures

Every page in this guide names a limit, threshold, fee, or deadline in plain language and sends you here for the number itself. This is the one page that prints those numbers, so there is one place to check and one place to update.

## How to read this page

The figures below are grouped by the part of the guide that refers to them, and each row carries the value, the year it applies to, and the primary source it came from. The page is updated once a year. A figure that is fixed by statute or by rule rather than reset annually says so in the Year column instead of carrying a year.

Where a row's Value reads "Varies by state," that is the answer, not a placeholder. This page does not hold a table of fifty state numbers and never will: the Source column instead points at what you consult to find your own state's figure, which is usually your state securities regulator, the model rule the states drew on, or the state office that charges the fee.

A Value that reads `NEEDS-VALUE` means the figure has not been established at all; the row is here so the gap is visible rather than silently missing.

<Callout type="info">
This guide is educational material about how independent advisory firms are established and run. It is not legal, compliance, tax, or investment advice, and it does not create an advisory or professional relationship. Registration requirements vary by state and change over time. Verify anything here against the current rules of your regulator, and get your own counsel before acting.
</Callout>
