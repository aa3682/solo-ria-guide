# Introduction

This guide explains how to leave a [broker-dealer](/glossary#broker-dealer) or larger advisory firm and build an independent registered investment advisory practice, from the first decision through the ongoing work of staying registered. It is free, public, and written in plain English.

## Who this guide is for

The first audience is someone who already works in the industry, most often at a broker-dealer or a larger advisory firm, and is thinking about going independent. The second audience is broader: anyone trying to decide whether the independent model fits them at all, before they take any concrete step. Both audiences read the same pages — the guide does not assume you have already decided to go independent, and the first step in [The Independent Path](/process) is written for exactly that question.

## The scenario this guide uses

Most of this guide follows one default scenario: an adviser registering at the state level rather than with the [SEC](/glossary#securities-and-exchange-commission), because the assets under management fall under the threshold that would require SEC registration. See [This year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures) for the current number. State registration is the more common path for a new independent practice, and it keeps the guide concrete rather than abstract. Where the SEC-registered path works differently, the relevant page says so and flags the divergence rather than silently assuming state registration throughout.

## Where this comes from

This guide is built from public primary sources — statutes, regulations, and agency publications — read alongside years working in and around the industry. It is not written from the perspective of someone who has personally completed a registration, and it says so wherever that distinction matters. Treat it as a map assembled from public records and observation, not as a first-hand account.

## How to use this guide

The site is organized around a distinction worth understanding before you start reading. [The Independent Path](/process) is a sequence of steps: they run in chronological order, and each one finishes before the work of the next begins. [Practice Areas](/domains) are different — they are standing bodies of knowledge, like compliance or firm economics, that never finish. You keep working in them for as long as the firm exists. Step pages link out to the areas that apply at that point in the sequence rather than repeating that material, so if you want the full picture of one area, read its page directly rather than piecing it together from the steps.

If you are new to the subject, start with [The Independent Path](/process) and read the steps in order. If you have a specific question about one subject, go straight to the relevant page under [Practice Areas](/domains).
