# The Independent Path

Going independent is not one decision; it is a sequence of them, and each step in that sequence produces something the next step needs. This section walks through that sequence from the first question — whether independence fits at all — through the ongoing work of staying registered once the firm is running. Twelve steps make up the full path, and each one gets its own page.

## Steps, not areas

The Independent Path is organized differently from [Practice Areas](/domains). A step is chronological: it has a beginning and an end, and once you have completed it, you move to the next one. Deciding whether independence fits happens once. Forming the business happens once. Filing your registration and getting approved happens once, at least for the initial registration. Areas are different — subjects like compliance, technology, or firm economics that apply throughout the sequence and never finish, because you keep working in them for as long as the firm exists. Each step page links to the areas that matter at that point, rather than repeating what those area pages already cover, so if a step touches compliance, you follow the link to read the full picture instead of finding it retold in shorter form.

## Why the order matters

Skipping ahead tends to cost more than it saves. Deciding on your advice approach before deciding whether independence fits at all means redoing that decision later, once the answer to the first question turns out to matter more than expected. Filing your registration before your disclosure documents and policies are written means the filing gets delayed or rejected, because a regulator reviewing the filing expects to see those documents already in place. The order below is not arbitrary — each step depends on decisions made in the one before it, and later steps generally assume the earlier ones are settled.

## The steps

1. [Decide If Independence Fits](/process/decide-if-independence-fits): Weigh whether the independent model suits your situation before committing to anything.
2. [Design the Firm](/process/design-the-firm): Sketch the shape of the practice — who it serves and how it makes money.
3. [Set Your Advice Approach](/process/set-your-advice-approach): Decide what kind of advice you will give and how you will deliver it.
4. [Form the Business](/process/form-the-business): Create the legal entity the advisory practice will operate through.
5. [Choose Your Regulator](/process/choose-your-regulator): Work out whether you will register with your state or with the SEC.
6. [Qualify as an Adviser](/process/qualify-as-an-adviser): Meet the individual requirements to act as an investment adviser representative.
7. [Write the Disclosure Documents](/process/write-the-disclosure-documents): Draft the documents that tell clients and regulators how the firm operates.
8. [Write the Policies](/process/write-the-policies): Put the firm's internal compliance and supervisory policies in writing.
9. [File and Get Approved](/process/file-and-get-approved): Submit the registration filing and work through the review process.
10. [Set Up Custody and Technology](/process/set-up-custody-and-technology): Choose the systems that hold client assets and run the practice.
11. [Leave and Transition Clients](/process/leave-and-transition-clients): Depart the prior firm and move clients to the new one.
12. [Stay Registered and Compliant](/process/stay-registered-and-compliant): Keep up with the filings and reviews that keep the registration current.
