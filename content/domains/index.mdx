# Practice Areas

A Practice Area is a body of knowledge, not a task. Where a step in [The Independent Path](/process) has a beginning and an end, a Practice Area never finishes — it is a subject you keep working in for as long as the firm operates. Compliance does not get "done" once the firm is registered; it is a standing concern that shows up at nearly every step. Firm economics does not stop mattering once the doors are open, and client education does not stop once the first client signs on. That is why these subjects live apart from the steps: a step page links to the area pages that apply at that point rather than restating what those pages already explain in full.

Nine areas cover the full range of standing subject matter this guide describes, from how the firm prices its work to how it manages the risks it carries. Each area below stands on its own, written so it can be read without the rest of the guide. If you have a question about one subject — pricing, technology, marketing — you can go straight to that page without reading the whole guide in order, and if you are working through the steps in sequence, you will meet each area again through the links on the step pages that need it.

## The areas

- [Service and Pricing Models](/domains/service-and-pricing-models): How the firm decides what it offers and what it charges for it.
- [Advice and Planning Process](/domains/advice-and-planning-process): The process the firm uses to turn a client's situation into a recommendation.
- [Technology and Client Experience](/domains/technology-and-client-experience): The systems that run the practice and what clients see and use.
- [Client Education](/domains/client-education): How the firm helps clients understand their own plan and the markets it depends on.
- [Marketing and Growth](/domains/marketing-and-growth): How the firm finds and reaches new clients.
- [Compliance and Supervision](/domains/compliance-and-supervision): The rules that govern the firm's conduct and how it oversees itself.
- [Records and Documentation](/domains/records-and-documentation): What the firm has to keep, for how long, and why.
- [Firm Economics](/domains/firm-economics): How the practice supports itself financially over time.
- [Risk and Insurance](/domains/risk-and-insurance): The risks an independent firm carries and how it manages them.
